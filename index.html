Why We Built CapCrush

Two of the biggest bottlenecks in modern video editing solved in a single native workflow.

1. Eliminating Hours of Manual Caption Work

Video editors spend hours manually transcribing, syncing, and styling subtitles for every video. CapCrush automates the entire process inside Premiere Pro—giving you complete flexibility with AI transcription, .srt import, or manual caption entry, all editable in one place.

2. Visual Storytelling with Automatic B-Roll

YouTubers, creators, and podcasters love showing visual representations on screen of what they are talking about. CapCrush lets you enable B-roll directly during caption generation, automatically surfacing relevant images and video clips to keep viewers hooked.

3-Step Workflow — Straight Into Your Premiere Timeline

No external browser tabs, no messy XML roundtrips, and zero hassle for editors.

1

Choose or Create a Template

Pick from a rich library of built-in preset templates with dual-color styling, or create and add your own custom templates anytime—backed by 100+ bundled fonts.

2

Generate Captions & Enable B-Roll

Transcribe active sequence audio with AI, import an existing .srt file, or write captions manually—and toggle AI B-Roll on to generate matching visual assets in the same run.

3

Live Audio/Video Review & Timeline Drop

Verify every word with live audio review and live visual preview, switch between Line by Line or Word by Word modes, and drop the output straight into Premiere Pro's own timeline.
